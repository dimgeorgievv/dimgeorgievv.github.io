About Publications News

Dimitar Georgiev, PhD

I am an AI researcher working at the intersection of machine learning, biology, chemistry and medicine.

I’m currently a postdoc in Prof. Dame Molly Stevens’ group at the University of Oxford. I develop machine learning methods to understand and engineer biological and chemical systems, with applications in cellular and tissue imaging, diagnostics, drug delivery and therapeutic design.

I obtained a PhD in AI & ML from Imperial College London in 2025. During my PhD, I developed machine learning methods and open-source software for Raman spectroscopy, demonstrating label-free biochemical imaging in neural organoids to study early human brain development.

Previously, I earned an MMath in Mathematics from the University of Southampton, where I worked as a Research Assistant on multi-agent systems and agent-based modelling. I minored in Computer Science and have industry experience in machine learning, data science and software engineering.
